Cover Dash Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026 · Shadey Prods

Cover Dash is a game by Shadey Prods ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses. In short, we don't ask for your name, email or any account. Your scores and settings stay on your device.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your high score, settings, skins, medals, items, tokens and local leaderboard on your phone or in your browser. This data is not sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing browser data deletes it.

Advertising

The app version shows optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. Ads only play when you choose to watch one. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and approximate location, and device information. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your phone settings. In regions that require it, the game asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. Learn more in how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your payment details. We only receive confirmation of what was bought so the game can unlock it.

Online leaderboards

If online leaderboards are added, the game will send your score, a player nickname, your country or region, and an anonymous player ID to our leaderboard service so scores can be ranked. This policy will be updated before that happens.

Children

Cover Dash is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and post the new version on this page.
